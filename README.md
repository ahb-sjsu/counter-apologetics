# Counter-Apologetics

This repository hosts essays and draft papers exploring logic, mathematics, theism, and Christian apologetics from a critical and textual-analytical perspective.

## Current contents

- **A Pragmatist Rebuttal to Logical and Metaphysical Arguments for God** (`A Pragmatist Rebuttal to Logical and Metaphysical Arguments for God.pdf`)  
  A detailed response to arguments used by apologists like William Lane Craig:
  - Kalam Cosmological Argument  
  - Contingency Argument  
  - Moral Argument  
  - Ontological Argument  

  The paper:
  - Treats logic and mathematics as **tools** rather than metaphysical mirrors.  
  - Uses **Gödel’s incompleteness theorems** to argue no formal system can guarantee metaphysical conclusions.  
  - Uses **quantum theory** to show that classical logic and causation are domain-limited, not universally binding.

- **The Problem of Davidic Descent: Why Jesus of Nazareth Cannot Fulfill the Messianic Prophecy of 2 Samuel 7:12** (`davidic_descent_paper.pdf`)
  A textual and linguistic analysis arguing that Jesus cannot satisfy the Hebrew Bible's requirement of Davidic descent:
  - The Hebrew word **zera** (seed) demands biological, paternal descent — which the virgin birth doctrine eliminates.
  - Textual evidence in Luke 1:36 suggests **Mary was of Levitical, not Davidic, lineage**.
  - Joseph's genealogy passes through **Jeconiah**, whose line is cursed from the Davidic throne (Jeremiah 22:24–30).
  - These three independent problems form a mutually reinforcing double bind for the Christian position.

- **The Temple Incident and the Problem of Sinlessness** (`temple_cleansing_paper.docx`)
  A Mosaic Law analysis of the Cleansing of the Temple, arguing it undermines the doctrine of Christ's sinlessness:
  - Christian scripture explicitly places Jesus **under the Mosaic Law** during his earthly ministry.
  - The Mosaic Law **prohibits assault and property destruction** without legitimate judicial authority.
  - The temple incident — recorded in all four Gospels — constitutes precisely such prohibited conduct.
  - Surveys standard apologetic responses (righteous anger, divine authority, prophetic action, Phinehas precedent) and argues each fails or collapses into **divine command theory**.
  - Explores the **Euthyphro dilemma** as it applies to claims of Jesus's moral perfection.
